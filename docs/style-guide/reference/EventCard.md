# Event card

The sheet laid on the chart when the voyage pauses: a `heading`, a log entry in the `log` style, and two to four choices with their stated consequences.

- `vellum-deep` with `shadow-paper`, the only floating surface in the game.
- Every choice shows its cost or reward on the right; risky choices use `vermilion`, money uses `gilt-ink`.
- The voyage stays paused until a choice is made.
- Reference drawing; not yet a coded component.
